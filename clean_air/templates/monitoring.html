Automatic catch-up and notifications

Enabled sources are checked at every startup and while the app runs. Missed dates are replenished automatically, not discarded when the twelve-month window moves. The startup notice only acknowledges this work; it does not cancel it. Separate source outcomes remain in Alerts.

Optional desktop delivery works while the app runs even with the browser closed. Windows can suppress a popup; in-app history remains the reliable record.
