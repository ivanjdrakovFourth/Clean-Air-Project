UNDERSTAND THE ANSWER

An honest comparison, not a black box.

01

Independent source histories

EEA and OpenAQ each have their own local measurements, progress and archives. Names and addresses identify the contributing monitors; community sensors are labelled. Missing direct-Bulgarian readings are not replaced with EEA data.

02

Comparable times

Current results compare the same recent reported hour, not readings from unrelated times. Historical averages give each qualifying station and valid UTC day equal weight. Missing data is never zero.

03

A clear definition

Normally, the least pollution wins. Settings can instead find the cleanest day/hour, most days within a reference, or the largest relative improvement. These are labelled as different questions, not a universal score.

A reference, not a safety certificate. WHO daily guidance is {{ "%g" | format(settings.reference.pm25_daily) }} µg/m³ for PM2.5 and {{ "%g" | format(settings.reference.pm10_daily) }} µg/m³ for PM10, with an annual 99th-percentile convention. Our descriptive city statistics do not establish WHO compliance, legal compliance or an absence of health risk. Annual guidelines are not applied to arbitrary periods.
