UNDERSTAND THE ANSWER

An honest comparison, not a black box.

01

Real measurements

We collect validated historical and provisional current observations from the European Environment Agency. Searches use your local database, never a live third-party request.

02

Consistent averages

We use matching UTC days. Each qualifying physical station has equal daily weight; each valid day has equal period weight. Missing observations are never turned into zero.

03

Context that matters

We show coverage, excluded cities and daily-reference statistics. City-mapped stations include different station types; this is not a population-weighted exposure estimate.

A reference, not a safety certificate. WHO daily guidance is {{ "%g" | format(settings.reference.pm25_daily) }} µg/m³ for PM2.5 and {{ "%g" | format(settings.reference.pm10_daily) }} µg/m³ for PM10, with an annual 99th-percentile convention. Our descriptive city statistics do not establish WHO compliance, legal compliance or an absence of health risk. Annual guidelines are not applied to arbitrary periods.
